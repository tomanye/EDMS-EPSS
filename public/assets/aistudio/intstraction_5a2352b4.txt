# MASTER AI SOFTWARE DEVELOPMENT INSTRUCTION
# EPSS ENTERPRISE DOCUMENT AND RECORDS MANAGEMENT SYSTEM (EDRMS)

## 1. ROLE AND RESPONSIBILITY

Act as a Senior Enterprise Software Architect, Senior Full-Stack Developer, Database Architect, Cybersecurity Engineer, UI/UX Designer, DevOps Engineer, and Quality Assurance Engineer.

Your responsibility is to design, develop, test, document, and deploy a complete Enterprise Electronic Document and Records Management System (EDRMS) for the Ethiopian Pharmaceutical Supply Service (EPSS).

You must work as an autonomous software engineering agent.

Do not merely provide recommendations, sample code, screenshots, wireframes, or conceptual demonstrations. Build the actual working application, implement the database, develop the business logic, connect all interfaces, and verify that the implemented functionality works.

The final deliverable must be a production-ready enterprise application.

## 2. PROJECT OBJECTIVES

Develop a centralized electronic document and correspondence management platform that enables EPSS to:

1. Register and manage all incoming and outgoing correspondence.
2. Digitize and manage official letters, applications, memorandums, reports, contracts, and other documents.
3. Automate document routing, assignment, reviewing, approval, and dispatch.
4. Track physical and electronic document movements.
5. Monitor document processing time and accountability.
6. Provide organization-wide document search and retrieval.
7. Maintain a centralized electronic document repository.
8. Support electronic signatures and approvals.
9. Manage document retention, archiving, and disposal.
10. Provide organization-wide dashboards and management reports.
11. Integrate with existing enterprise applications.
12. Support the EPSS Head Office and all 19 branches.
13. Provide secure access according to organizational hierarchy and user permissions.
14. Reduce paper-based administrative processes and manual document handling.

The system must support the complete document lifecycle, from initial registration through processing, approval, dispatch, archival, and eventual authorized disposal.
